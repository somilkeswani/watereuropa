What problem is Water Europa trying to solve?

Kashmore faces serious challenges with access to safe drinking water. Groundwater in the area is highly saline, while chemical dumping has also contributed to concerns about local water quality.

For many families, accessing usable drinking water can require traveling 20km to the nearest water source and transporting water back home or purchasing expensive branded water. This makes access difficult, especially for rural communities with limited infrastructure and affordability.

OUR BEGINNINGS

Why Water Europa Started

The project operates a community-level reverse osmosis (RO) water facility in Kashmore and focuses on making safe drinking water more accessible to local families.

How did the facility come together?

The facility operates from a space provided by my uncle in Kashmore, which allowed us to establish the project without having to take on a separate rental cost for the location.

What does the Water Europa facility do?

The facility uses reverse osmosis and filtration to treat local groundwater and produce purified drinking water.

From groundwater to drinking water: here is how the plant treats it, step by step, and what the results look like.

TREATMENT PROCESS

How the water is treated

At Water Europa, our water treatment process begins by collecting groundwater in a storage tank.

  1. 1

    Carbon filtration

    The water then passes through carbon-filtering vessels, which help remove impurities.

  2. 2

    Sediment filtration

    A series of pipes carry it through sediment filtration to remove accumulated dirt and suspended particles.

  3. 3

    Reverse Osmosis (RO)

    Next, water pumps send the filtered water into the Reverse Osmosis (RO) system, which uses specialised membranes, imported from the USA and supplied by Toray Membrane USA, Inc., to remove dissolved salts and other impurities.

shield

An automatic dosing pump injects antiscalant into the system to prevent mineral deposits from accumulating on the RO membranes, helping maintain filtration efficiency.

  1. 4

    Storage & antibacterial filter

    Finally, the treated water is stored and passed through an additional antibacterial rod filter before distribution to the local community.

Water quality (TDS)

Our plant uses groundwater with an initial Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) level of approximately 2200 ppm. The RO membrane rejects approximately 95 to 97% of the dissolved salts, lowering the TDS level to around 120 to 130 ppm, depending on the season. During June and July, the treated water’s TDS level can fall to approximately 60 to 70 ppm.
